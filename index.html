Daynest — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026 App: Daynest (com.orvexa.daynest) Contact: orvexa-support@googlegroups.com

Daynest is a personal finance and planning app. This policy explains what the app collects, where it is kept, and what you can do about it.

1. The short version

  • Your records — transactions, amounts, notes, categories, accounts, budgets, calendar events, tasks and notes — are stored on your own device.
  • With Pro, your records are uploaded to the cloud only when you tap "Sync now" yourself. There is no background or automatic record upload.
  • Receipt photos are read on your device and are never uploaded.
  • There are no advertising or app-usage analytics SDKs in this app. Subscriptions are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat, which process your purchase information (see section 2.4). Google's on-device text-recognition library may send technical diagnostics to Google (see section 2.5).
  • You can delete your Daynest account and synced records inside the app. Store purchase history and RevenueCat records have separate deletion rules (see section 6).

2. What we collect

2.1 Account information

Signing in is required to use Daynest. There are three ways to sign in, and all of them are handled by Google's Firebase Authentication:

  • Google
  • Apple (iOS only)
  • An email address and a password you choose

In every case the account we hold consists of a user ID that identifies you, your email address, and — where the provider supplies one — your display name.

If you sign in with Google or Apple we never receive your password for those accounts. If you register with an email address and password, that password is sent directly to Firebase Authentication, which stores it in hashed form; it is never visible to us and is never written to our own records.

Registering with an email address also means Firebase sends you a verification email, and a reset email if you ask to change your password. You cannot use the app until the address is verified, so that we know the address belongs to you.

If you use Sign in with Apple, you may choose "Hide My Email". We then only ever see Apple's relay address, not your real one.

2.2 Your records

Everything you enter in the app:

  • transactions (amount, date, time, note, category, account, ledger),
  • categories, accounts, ledgers and budgets,
  • reimbursement records and the people named on them,
  • calendar events, tasks and reminders,
  • notes,
  • your app settings (language, theme, date format, display options).

This is written to a database on your device. It is sent off the device only through sync — see section 3.

2.3 Receipt photos

If you use receipt scanning, Daynest asks for permission to use your camera or to pick an image from your photo library.

The photo is processed entirely on your device, by the text recognition built into the operating system (Apple Vision on iOS, Google ML Kit on Android — the on-device version, bundled inside the app).

The image is not uploaded anywhere and is not stored by Daynest. Only the text values you confirm and save — such as the amount and the date — become part of your records.

2.4 Purchases and subscriptions

Plus and Pro are monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscriptions through Google Play Billing. Eligible new subscribers can receive a 7-day free trial. Google processes payment; Daynest never receives your card number or other payment-method details.

RevenueCat, Inc. manages subscription status, purchase verification and restoring access. When you sign in, Daynest sends your Firebase Authentication user ID to RevenueCat as your customer ID. Before sign-in, the SDK may create an anonymous customer ID. Daynest does not send your ledger records, receipt images or account email to RevenueCat for billing.

Google Play and RevenueCat process purchase information such as the product, purchase token, purchase and expiry times, price and currency, transaction country, and renewal or cancellation status. Play's real-time developer notifications help RevenueCat keep subscription status up to date. The SDK also processes technical app and device information, such as app and OS versions, device type/model and locale. RevenueCat may derive a country from your IP address; this is separate from accessing GPS or precise location. These services may communicate when the app starts or subscription status refreshes, without you tapping "Sync now". This does not upload your records.

Daynest stores the last known subscription tier locally, per account, in SharedPreferences so paid features can remain available offline. RevenueCat also maintains its own SDK cache.

2.5 What we do not collect

Daynest does not collect or use:

  • your GPS or precise location,
  • your contacts, calendar (the system one), call log or messages,
  • advertising identifiers,
  • general app-usage analytics or advertising profiles,
  • app crash reports through a crash-reporting SDK.

There are no advertising SDKs or general app-usage analytics SDKs in this app. Subscription reporting by RevenueCat is separate from tracking how you use your finance records. Google's Android ML Kit SDK may collect technical information, installation identifiers and performance or feature events for SDK diagnostics and usage analytics. Receipt images and recognized text remain on-device during recognition; only values you save may later be synced.

3. Where your data is stored

3.1 On your device

By default, your finance and planning records stay in a local database on your phone. Subscription status is cached separately as described in section 2.4. Sign-in, account profile creation and subscription services still use the network even if you never sync your records. Firebase also holds an account profile (your user ID, email and signup time).

If you choose to export a backup or transfer attachment photos to another phone over local Wi-Fi, those records or photos leave this device at your request. Attachment photos are not part of cloud sync; cloud photo storage is not available in v2.

3.2 Cloud sync — Pro, only when you ask for it

Daynest has a manual sync for Pro subscribers. When you open the account screen and tap "Sync now", a copy of your records is sent to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and stored under your own account, so you can restore it or move to another device. It is stored under users/{uid}, where uid is your Firebase user ID.

  • Sync never runs on its own, on a schedule, or in the background.
  • The copy is stored in an area private to your account and is not readable by other users of the app.
  • Transfer is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS), and Google encrypts the stored data at rest.

Their own policies apply to what they do: Google privacy policy · RevenueCat privacy policy · Apple privacy policy

Google's ML Kit data disclosure explains the technical data its Android SDK processes.

3.4 Where in the world

Your synced copy is stored in Google's Singapore region (asia-southeast1). Sign-in accounts are held by Firebase Authentication, which Google may operate from data centres in other countries. Google applies its own safeguards to such transfers. RevenueCat describes storage in the United States in its privacy policy. Subscription processing is not limited to the Firestore sync region.

4. Why we are allowed to hold it

Where the GDPR or a similar law applies to you:

  • Performance of a contract — we need your account information and your records to give you the app you asked for, and purchase information to provide and restore the subscription you choose.
  • Consent — receipt scanning uses your camera or photos only after you allow it, and sync only runs when you press the button. You can withdraw either at any time, by revoking the permission in your system settings or by simply not syncing.

5. How long we keep it

  • On your device: until you delete the record, or delete the app.
  • In Firestore: your synced records stay until replaced through sync or removed through account deletion. If Pro expires, sync stops but existing local and cloud records are kept; account deletion remains available at every tier.
  • Local subscription cache: the last known tier stays in local app preferences until updated or app data is cleared. Deleting the Daynest account does not explicitly clear this per-account cache.
  • Subscription records: Google and RevenueCat retain records under their own policies, including where needed for accounting, refunds, disputes or legal obligations. We do not set a fixed retention period for their records.

6. Deleting your account and your data

Step-by-step instructions, and exactly what is removed, are on a separate page: Deleting your Daynest account and data.

Open Account → Delete account in the app. You will be asked to sign in again to confirm it is you. Daynest then deletes your synced copy and your sign-in account, regardless of whether you have Free, Plus or Pro. The current app removes the Firestore sync snapshot and replaces the account profile with a minimal deletion marker to prevent other devices from uploading again. The marker holds only your user ID and a "deleted" flag; email us if you want it removed as well.

Deleting your Daynest account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play's subscription management screen if you do not want it to renew. Daynest cannot erase Google's purchase history.

The current account-deletion flow does not delete the RevenueCat customer record; signing out is not deletion. To request removal of that record, email orvexa-support@googlegroups.com. We can request or carry out customer deletion through RevenueCat, subject to any records it must retain. This does not remove Google's purchase history or cancel your subscription.

Deleting the app alone does not delete a copy you previously synced — use the in-app deletion for that.

If you cannot reach the in-app option, email orvexa-support@googlegroups.com from the address on the account and we will delete it for you.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to its use, or to complain to your data protection authority.

Most of this you can do yourself in the app: your records are all visible and editable, and deletion is built in. For anything else, write to orvexa-support@googlegroups.com and we will reply within 30 days.

8. Security

Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication. We never see or store your password: a Google or Apple password never reaches us at all, and a Daynest password goes straight to Firebase, which keeps only a hashed form of it. Data sent to Firebase and RevenueCat is encrypted in transit with TLS. Optional phone-to-phone attachment transfer uses a local Wi-Fi connection and a pairing code, without app-level transport encryption. Use a trusted network for that transfer. Your synced copy is restricted to your own account by server-side rules.

No system is perfectly secure. The local database on your phone is protected by your device's own lock and encryption, so please keep a screen lock on.

9. Children

Daynest is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will remove it.

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, tell you in the app.
