Deleting your Daynest account and data

App: Daynest (com.orvexa.daynest) Developer: Orvexa Contact: orvexa-support@googlegroups.com Last updated: 16 September 2026

This page explains how to delete your Daynest account, how to delete only part of your data without closing your account, and exactly what is removed in each case.

Delete your whole account

You can do this yourself, inside the app:

  1. Open Daynest and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (the last tab) → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom of the page.
  4. Confirm in the dialog that appears.
  5. Sign in once more when asked. This is a security step required by the sign-in provider so that only the account owner can delete the account.

The deletion runs immediately. When it finishes you are returned to the sign-in screen, and the account no longer exists.

If you cannot open the app

Email orvexa-support@googlegroups.com from the email address the account is registered under, with the subject Delete my Daynest account. We use the sending address to confirm ownership, and we reply within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Deleting the account removes all of the following:

  • Your sign-in account — the account held by Firebase Authentication, including the user ID, the email address, the display name, and, for email sign-ups, the stored password hash.
  • Your synced copy in the cloud — every record uploaded to Cloud Firestore under your account: transactions, categories, accounts, ledgers, budgets, reimbursement records, calendar events, tasks, reminders and notes.
  • The data on the device you are using — the local database is cleared and scheduled reminder notifications are cancelled.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Nothing is kept by Daynest. We run no separate backups, no archive and no analytics store, so there is no additional retention period. The deletion is immediate and cannot be undone — we cannot restore an account or its records afterwards.
  • Google operates Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore on our behalf. Residual copies may persist briefly inside Google’s own infrastructure until its routine processes overwrite them; that is governed by Google’s privacy policy, not by us, and such copies are not reachable by Daynest or by anyone else.
  • Other devices you installed Daynest on keep whatever is in their own local database until you open the app there, or uninstall it. The cloud copy is already gone, so nothing can be restored from it.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete a copy you previously synced. Use the in-app deletion above for that.

Delete only some of your data

You do not have to close your account to remove data. Everything you enter is editable and deletable inside the app:

  1. Open the record you want to remove — a transaction, category, account, ledger, budget, reimbursement record, calendar event, task or note.
  2. Tap Delete in the edit dialog and confirm.

That removes it from the device straight away. To remove it from your cloud copy as well, open Settings → Account and tap Sync now: the sync replaces the stored copy with the current contents of your device, so deleted records disappear from the cloud too.

If you would rather have us do it, email orvexa-support@googlegroups.com from the address on the account and tell us what to remove.
